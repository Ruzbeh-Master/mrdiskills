Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Master Research & Development Institute (MRDI) (“MRDI Skills,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data when you visit or use https://mrdiskills.com/ (the “Website”) or otherwise contact us through our online forms and communication channels.

MRDI Skills provides hands-on automotive and EV engineering training, job placement support, and technical project consultancy in Surat, Gujarat. This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our Enquiry Form, Project Enquiry Form, footer newsletter signup, WhatsApp-based submission flow, and any related pages, features, or services offered through the Website.

By using the Website, submitting your information, or otherwise interacting with us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where required under applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), your consent will be obtained before or at the time of collecting your personal data.

For the purposes of the DPDP Act, MRDI Skills acts as a “Data Fiduciary,” and you, as an identifiable individual to whom the personal data relates, are the “Data Principal.”

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. The categories of personal data we may collect include:

A. Information You Provide Directly

When you fill out our enquiry or project forms, subscribe to updates, or communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Enquiry topic or program interest
  • Project details, scope, requirements, or other information you choose to share
  • Any additional information submitted in your message, including preferences, location, academic background, or career goals, if included by you

B. Information Collected Through Website Use

When you browse the Website, we may collect:

  • Usage analytics
  • Device and browser-related information
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address
  • Cookie and similar tracking data
  • Pages visited, time spent, clicks, referral source, and interaction patterns

C. Information Received Through Third-Party Platforms

If you interact with us via embedded or linked third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Reviews/Maps, YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, or external partner links such as ISAC Systems, those platforms may independently collect information about you in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data unless you voluntarily provide it in a message or form submission. Please avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive information unless it is required for your specific enquiry.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following business and operational purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our website or via WhatsApp
  • To assess eligibility and interest for our 6-Month Skill Program, PSP Program, Online Sessions, and related training offerings
  • To understand project requirements and provide project consultancy scoping, quotations, or follow-up communication
  • To support job placement and candidate engagement initiatives
  • To send newsletters, service updates, event notices, and course-related information where you have opted in
  • To contact you regarding admissions, counselling, scheduling, or administrative matters
  • To improve the Website, our training offerings, and user experience
  • To monitor Website performance, usage trends, and campaign effectiveness through analytics
  • To maintain internal records, business communications, and compliance documentation
  • To prevent misuse, fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access
  • To comply with applicable law, lawful requests, or enforce our rights

Where required under the DPDP Act, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent and/or for purposes that are reasonably expected in connection with your interaction with us. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or operational limitations.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information only as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy and in accordance with applicable law.

A. WhatsApp

Our forms route submissions directly through WhatsApp (“WhatsApp Us”) so that our team can review and respond quickly. When you choose to contact us via WhatsApp, your information may be processed by WhatsApp and Meta Platforms, Inc. according to their own terms and privacy practices. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp’s privacy policy before using that channel.

B. Google Services

We may use Google Analytics and Google Maps/Google Reviews integrations to understand Website usage and display reviews or location-related content. Google may collect information such as usage data, device identifiers, and cookie-related data subject to its own privacy policy.

C. Email Marketing Providers

If you subscribe to our newsletter or request updates, we may use service providers such as Mailchimp or SendGrid to send emails on our behalf. These providers may process your name, email address, and engagement data strictly to deliver communications and maintain service performance.

D. Social Media and Embedded Platforms

The Website may include links to or embedded content from YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and similar services. These platforms may collect data independently when you interact with their content.

E. Business and Technical Service Providers

We may use trusted vendors for website hosting, security, analytics, communication, and maintenance. Such providers are permitted to process personal data only as needed to perform services for MRDI Skills and must safeguard the information appropriately.

F. Legal and Regulatory Disclosures

We may disclose personal data when required to do so by law, regulation, court order, government authority, or to protect our legal rights, users, or the public.

Where third parties process your personal data, they may act as separate data fiduciaries or as processors depending on the service and legal arrangement. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before engaging with them.

5. Data Storage and Security

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, to comply with legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce agreements.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. These measures may include access controls, secure hosting practices, role-based access, and limited internal access to data.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is fully secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for using secure channels and exercising caution when sharing information online. If you submit information through third-party platforms such as WhatsApp or social media, your data may also be subject to those services’ security practices.

6. Your Rights Under Applicable Law

Subject to the DPDP Act, 2023 and any applicable legal exceptions, you as a Data Principal may have the following rights:

  • Right to access information about your personal data being processed
  • Right to correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal data
  • Right to erasure of personal data no longer necessary for the stated purpose, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time for processing based on consent
  • Right to grievance redressal through our designated contact point
  • Right to nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, where recognized under applicable law

To exercise these rights, please contact us at mrdi.development@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Please note that withdrawing consent may affect our ability to contact you, process your enquiry, provide course information, or deliver newsletter updates. Some information may still be retained where required by law, accounting, contractual, security, or recordkeeping obligations.

You may manage cookies through your browser settings and, where available, through on-site consent tools. Disabling cookies may limit some features or affect the Website’s performance.

Our embedded third-party services and social media links may also place cookies or tracking technologies on your device when you interact with them. Their use of cookies is governed by their own policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended primarily for individuals seeking automotive, EV engineering, skill development, placement support, and project consultancy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate consent or legal basis as required under applicable law.

If you are under the age where consent is legally required under applicable Indian law, please use the Website only with the involvement and consent of a parent or lawful guardian. If we become aware that personal data of a child has been collected without proper authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it promptly.

If you believe a child has provided us personal data without appropriate consent, please contact us immediately at mrdi.development@gmail.com.

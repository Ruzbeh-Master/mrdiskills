Project Consultancy & Product Development

Need engineering project consultancy in Surat or support developing a product anywhere in India? MRDI helps organizations, institutions and innovators clarify scope, explore technical options and plan practical next steps toward a prototype or product.

Support shaped around your project

From the first brief to the next milestone

MRDI provides project consultancy and product development support from Surat, Gujarat. We begin by understanding your objective, current stage, users, technical requirements and constraints. If the project is a fit, we discuss scope, deliverables, timeline, resources and commercial terms before work begins.

MRDI's demonstrated project experience includes automotive and electric mobility, prototyping, fabrication, and student engineering projects. For other domains, share your brief so feasibility and fit can be discussed case by case.

A clear starting point

How to begin a project with MRDI

  1. Send a short description of the project or problem.
  2. Tell us the current stage, the support you are seeking, and your target timeline.
  3. MRDI will review the enquiry and discuss a suitable scope and next steps.

You do not need a polished proposal to start the conversation. MRDI reviews the brief and follows up to discuss fit and possible next steps. Please avoid including confidential or sensitive information in the initial enquiry.

Project consultancy questions

Frequently asked questions

What project consultancy does MRDI provide?

MRDI discusses project scoping, engineering design, product development, prototyping, fabrication, testing and student project support. The suitable scope depends on your goals, technical requirements and resources.

Can MRDI help develop a product from an idea?

Yes. Share your idea, intended users, current stage and constraints. MRDI can review the brief and discuss whether product development or prototype support is a suitable fit.

What kinds of projects has MRDI supported?

Examples include the ARKA SAE Mini Baja prototype with hands-on student training, product development for Nutrava Wellness Foods, and Azvina electric bicycle and electric kick scooter products.

Where is MRDI located, and which areas does it serve?

MRDI is based in Surat, Gujarat, India. Organizations, institutions and innovators can contact MRDI from Surat and other locations to discuss project consultancy.

How do I request a project consultation?

Use the project enquiry form on this page or contact MRDI by WhatsApp at +91 8905636766 or email mrdi.development@gmail.com. Include your project stage, support needed and target timeline.

How does MRDI define project scope, cost and timeline?

MRDI first reviews the project objective, requirements, constraints and requested support. Deliverables, resources, schedule and commercial terms can then be discussed before work begins.

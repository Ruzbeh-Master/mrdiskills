08|Programs

Questions, answered.

Find out who can join, how the programs differ, and how to get started.

Explore the programs ↗
Who can join MRDI programs?

Students, diploma holders, and early-career learners interested in automotive, EV, and practical engineering skills can join MRDI programs.

What is the difference between the 6-Month Program and PSP Program?

The 6-Month Skill Development Program focuses on structured automotive learning and workshop readiness, while the PSP Program focuses on prototyping and innovation-oriented project execution.

How do I enroll in a program?

You can enroll by submitting an enquiry through the Enquiry page or by contacting MRDI directly through phone or WhatsApp.
